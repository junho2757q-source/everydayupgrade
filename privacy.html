Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06

This policy covers “Everyday Upgrade Kami” (the “App”), an internal tool operated by Everyday Upgrade (the “Store”).

What the App does

The App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to publish the Store’s own brand videos to the Store’s own TikTok account (@shopeverydayupgrade), after a human on our team reviews and approves each post.

Scopes we request

  • user.info.basic: to confirm which TikTok account is connected (the Store’s own account).
  • video.upload: to send our own video files to that account.
  • video.publish: to publish our own approved videos on that account.

Whose data we handle

Only the Store’s own TikTok account is connected. We do not collect data from any other TikTok user, and the App is not offered to the public.

How we store data

The access token for the Store’s account is stored server-side only. It is never placed in web pages, browser storage, or public code.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell or share any data obtained through TikTok.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data held by the App, or to ask a privacy question, email sochi3515324@gmail.com. We will delete the stored token and related data and may disconnect the App from the account. You can also revoke the App’s access at any time in your TikTok account settings.
