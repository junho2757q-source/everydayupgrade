Everyday Upgrade Kami

Everyday Upgrade Kami is an internal tool used by Everyday Upgrade, a US home-furniture store (“Daily Made Easy”), to publish its own brand videos to its own TikTok account (@shopeverydayupgrade). Every post is reviewed and approved by a human on our team before it is published. The app is not offered to the public and does not access any other person’s TikTok account.
